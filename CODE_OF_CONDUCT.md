# Code of conduct

Zero Slop should be a useful place to discuss writing, tooling, and evidence.
Treat contributors and users with respect regardless of background, identity,
experience, or communication style.

Be specific about text and behavior. Do not attack a person, speculate about
who wrote a draft, or use the score to police authorship. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized conduct, and publishing another person's private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these rules and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For a private conduct
report, use the contact route at [zero-slop.ai/about](https://zero-slop.ai/about/)
and avoid including confidential draft text unless it is necessary.
